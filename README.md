### About the Experiment 

This experiment explores the classic Lotka-Volterra mathematical model to show how predator and prey populations continuously influence each other over time. By adjusting growth, death, and hunting rates in the simulator, you will observe the endless cycle of "boom and bust" that keeps these two species in a delicate natural balance.
